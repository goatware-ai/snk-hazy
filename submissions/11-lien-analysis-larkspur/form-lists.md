# Form lists

Entries for the platform form, written from the zip listings. Each Input File List entry is the exact file name, a plain hyphen, then what it contains.

## Input File List

- practice_note_pn14_mechanics_liens.docx - the firm's practice note on mechanics lien claims on private works: preliminary notice, completion and the notice of completion, the time to record, the amount of the lien, waivers and releases, the 90 day enforcement period, the owner's remedies, and the counting conventions the office uses
- claims_of_lien_larkspur_crossing.docx - the seven claims of mechanics lien recorded against the property, transcribed from the recorder's copies with each proof of service affidavit and the exhibits two claimants attached
- owner_project_file_larkspur.xlsx - the owner's project file: the preliminary notices received, the waivers and releases received with each payment application, the owner's payments to the direct contractor with retention, and the subcontractor list with subcontract values
- dk_subcontractor_payments.csv - the direct contractor's subcontractor payment ledger for the job, every check issued to a subcontractor with its payment application, amount, and bank status
- completion_and_correspondence_larkspur.docx - the notice of completion as recorded, the construction manager's completion memorandum, the email exchange among the owner, the direct contractor, and the firm, and the civil index and recorder searches

## Output File List

- lien_analysis_larkspur_crossing.xlsx - the lien analysis workbook with the page for the partner, the governing dates, the preliminary notice and deadline tests, the waivers read against the payment ledger, each claim built up to its enforceable amount, the standing and bond for each claim, and the note to the partner
