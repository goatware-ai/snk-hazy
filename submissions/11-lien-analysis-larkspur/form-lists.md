# Form lists

Entries for the platform form, written from the zip listings. Each Input File List entry is the exact file name, a plain hyphen, then what it contains.

## Input File List

- practice_note_pn14_mechanics_liens.docx - the firm's practice note on mechanics lien claims on private works: who is a direct contractor, preliminary notice, completion and the notice of completion and the copies the owner has to give, the time to record, service of the claim, the amount of the lien, the four waiver and release forms, the 90 day enforcement period, the owner's remedies, and the counting conventions the office uses
- claims_of_lien_larkspur_crossing.docx - the fifteen claims of mechanics lien recorded against the property, transcribed from the recorder's copies with each proof of service affidavit and the exhibits four claimants attached, three of them statements of account
- owner_project_file_larkspur.xlsx - the owner's project file: the preliminary notices received, the waivers and releases received with each payment application, the owner's payments to the direct contractor with retention and the approved change orders, the agreements the owner signed itself, the subcontractor list with subcontract values, and the outgoing certified mail log
- dk_subcontractor_payments.csv - the direct contractor's subcontractor payment ledger for the job, every check and stop payment entered against a subcontractor with its payment application, amount, and the date the check paid at the bank
- completion_and_correspondence_larkspur.docx - the notice of completion as recorded, the construction manager's two memoranda on completion and on the copies of the notice, the email exchange among the owner, the direct contractor, and the firm, and the civil index and recorder searches

## Output File List

- lien_analysis_larkspur_crossing.xlsx - the lien analysis workbook with the page for the partner and the two answers for the owner, the governing dates, the preliminary notice, notice of completion copy, and deadline tests, the waivers read against the payment ledger, each claim built up to its enforceable amount, the standing and bond for each claim, and the note to the partner
