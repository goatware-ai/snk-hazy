# Form lists

Entries for the platform form, written from the zip listings. Each Input File List entry is the exact file name, a plain hyphen, then what it contains.

## Input File List

- field_notes_traverse_tract7.csv - the crew's notes file: every traverse shot and every monument and fence tie, with date, time, station, point, quadrant bearing in degrees, minutes and seconds, horizontal distance in feet, and the point code
- field_book_tract7.docx - the field book for the job: client, purpose, dates, crew, instrument and prisms, basis of bearings, the traverse stations and how the loop was run, the monument found at each corner, the fence posts tied, and the crew's notes for the office
- deed_book_412_page_233.docx - the firm's transcription of the 1962 warranty deed with the metes and bounds calls on magnetic bearings and the recited acreage
- record_research_tract7.docx - the record research: chain of title with the 1978 deed of trust, assessor and annexation records, the adjoining deeds, and the notes of the 1994 and 2008 recorded surveys
- firm_survey_standards.docx - the firm's boundary survey standards: traverse reduction and closure, the closure standards, ties and corrections, area, deed comparison and rotation, priority of calls, original and later monuments, corners to be set, occupation lines, and what the office analysis must contain

## Output File List

- tract7_boundary_analysis.xlsx - the boundary analysis workbook with the findings page, the traverse reduction, the ties, the deed comparison, the corners, the area computation, the fences and the note to the sealing surveyor
