# Form lists

Entries for the platform form, written from the zip listings. Each Input File List entry is the exact file name, a plain hyphen, then what it contains.

## Input File List

- field_notes_traverse_tract7.csv - the crew's traverse shots on the five corners, forward and reverse on each course, with quadrant bearing in degrees, minutes and seconds and horizontal distance in feet
- field_book_tract7.docx - the field book for the job: client, purpose, dates, crew, equipment, basis of bearings, the monument recovery at each corner, the traverse method and the crew's notes for the office
- deed_book_412_page_233.docx - the firm's transcription of the 1962 warranty deed with the metes and bounds calls on magnetic bearings and the recited acreage
- firm_survey_standards.docx - the firm's boundary survey standards: traverse reduction and closure, the precision standard, area, deed comparison and conflict rules, priority of calls, and what the office analysis must contain

## Output File List

- tract7_boundary_analysis.xlsx - the boundary analysis workbook with the findings page, the traverse reduction, the deed comparison, the area computation and the note to the sealing surveyor
