# Form lists

Entries for the platform form, written from the zip listings. Each Input File List entry is the exact file name, a plain hyphen, then what it contains.

## Input File List

- pretreatment_permit_ip0421.docx - Wastewater Discharge Permit IP-0421 for Vandeveer Plating: effluent limitations, monitoring and computation rules, reporting and notification conditions, the significant noncompliance test, the signatory condition, bypass conditions, and the Attachment B report format and certification
- lab_results_apr_sep2026.xlsx - the laboratory's compiled results from the reports issued April through October 1, 2026, one row per sample with results and qualifiers by parameter, the chain of custody log with sampling points, analysis dates and report issue times, and the methods with detection limits and holding times
- lab_case_narratives_2026.docx - the laboratory's case narratives and report revisions for the same reports, covering qualified results, repeated analyses, held reports and a revised report
- operator_log_jul2026.docx - the pretreatment system operator's July 2026 log with the daily totalizer register readings, the in-line pH chart bands and grab readings, and the day's notes on sampling, the totalizer register replacement, a contained rinse tank overflow and calls
- operator_log_aug2026.docx - the pretreatment system operator's August 2026 log with the daily totalizer register readings, the in-line pH chart bands and grab readings, and the day's notes on sampling, the City's sampling crew and calls
- operator_log_sep2026.docx - the pretreatment system operator's September 2026 log with the daily totalizer register readings, the in-line pH chart bands and grab readings, and the notes on the rinse tank overflow, the acid dump, the notices made and the samples collected
- city_correspondence_oct2026.docx - the Pretreatment Coordinator's note on the third quarter report with the City's own sampling results, composites and a grab, and her telephone log for the quarter, and the plant manager's forwarding email to the consultant
