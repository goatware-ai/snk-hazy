# Form lists

Entries for the platform form, written from the zip listings. Each Input File List entry is the exact file name, a plain hyphen, then what it contains.

## Input File List

- cylinder_breaks_2417.csv - the laboratory's break record for the sets cast in August and September, one line per set with its cylinders across the line: test date and time, two measured diameters, maximum load and fracture type for each cylinder at every age, the technician who broke the set and the remarks written at the break
- field_sampling_log_2417.csv - the field sampling log keyed from the two field technicians' books, one line per set: date, time molded, location, delivery ticket, truck, mix design, batch time, slump, air content, concrete temperature, cylinder size and count, initial curing, curing box thermometer readings, pickup and remarks
- placement_log_2417.csv - the contractor's placement log with each placement's date, element, mix design, cubic yards, surface area of slab or wall, and start and finish times
- spec_033000_excerpt.docx - the project specification excerpt for cast-in-place concrete: mixtures and limits, sampling frequency, what makes a strength test, age tolerance, range, defects, curing and set-aside rules, the acceptance criteria and what follows a failing test, and the reporting form
- lab_procedure_lp07_c39.docx - the laboratory's compressive strength procedure: specimens, test ages and working days, how the field log and break record are kept, area and strength computation and rounding, within-test range, fracture types and reporting
- lab_quality_memo_2417.docx - the laboratory manager's memo on the events of the period that bear on the tests, and what the acceptance summary needs to carry
- project_correspondence_2417.docx - the correspondence the laboratory received or sent during the period, in date order, from the contractor, the ready mix supplier, the owner's representative, the laboratory and the engineer of record
- july_summary_transmittal_2417.docx - the laboratory's transmittal of the July acceptance summary, with the July strength tests of each mixture and their moving averages

## Output File List

- acceptance_summary_2417_aug_sep.xlsx - the acceptance summary workbook with the engineer's page, the cylinder strengths and ages, the strength tests with moving averages in the order the specification counts them, the sampling check by mixture and day, and the note to the laboratory manager
