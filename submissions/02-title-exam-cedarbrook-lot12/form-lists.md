# Form lists

Entries for the platform form, written from the zip listings. Each Input File List entry is the exact file name, a plain hyphen, then what it contains.

## Input File List

- recorder_index_cedarbrook_lot12.csv - Licking County recorder's index entries returned by the name and lot runs, 1986 through the October 2, 2026 search date: instrument number, recording date, document type, parties, volume and page, amount, legal description as indexed, and referenced instrument as indexed
- instrument_abstracts_hlt262214.docx - the searcher's abstracts of the recorded instruments, giving the parties, recitals, legal description, dates, and execution of each instrument as it appears on the recorded image
- title_order_hlt262214.docx - the closing agent's title order and closing instructions for order HLT-26-2214, with the parties, price, lender, commitment and closing dates, proration and interest instructions, the seller information sheet notes, and the underwriter's examination guideline excerpts
- tax_and_lien_search_hlt262214.xlsx - the treasurer's tax duplicate inquiry for the parcel, the clerk of courts judgment lien docket with the later entries filed under each certificate, and the names searched
- court_records_search_hlt262214.xlsx - the probate court index run (estates, guardianships, and marriage licenses) and the domestic relations case index run under the surnames in the chain
- location_survey_418_cedarbrook.docx - the buyers' mortgage location survey report of October 9, 2026, with the basis of the survey, the measured ties to each improvement, and the lines of occupation

## Output File List

- title_exam_report_cedarbrook_lot12.docx - the title examination report to the closing agent with vesting, chain of title, requirements, exceptions, closing figures, survey matters, matters examined and passed, and open matters
