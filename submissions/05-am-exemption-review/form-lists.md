# Form lists

Entries for the platform form, written from the zip listings. Each Input File List entry is the exact file name, a plain hyphen, then what it contains.

## Input File List

- position_descriptions_am.docx - the company's position descriptions for store manager, assistant store manager and sales associate, with the January 2024 revision notes on the assistant manager classification
- hr_policy_excerpts.docx - the classification policy with the salary and duties tests, the salary and standard workweek policy, the bonus plan, the badge record policy, and the wage and hour reference on look-back and exposure
- badge_hours_weekly_2024_2026.csv - weekly badge hours for the three salaried assistant managers over 104 workweeks, laid out in three side-by-side blocks of 35 weeks
- payroll_history_am.xlsx - the three assistant managers' salary history, quarterly bonus history, and the store staffing beside each of them by half-year
- demand_letter_voight.docx - the attorney's demand letter of October 2, 2026 on Kendra Voight's overtime, with its figures and its response date

## Output File List

- am_exemption_review_oct2026.xlsx - the exemption review workbook with the summary, the week-by-week back overtime for the misclassified assistant managers, the demand letter set against the review, and the note to the general counsel
