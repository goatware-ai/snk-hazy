# Form lists

Entries for the platform form, written from the zip listings. Each Input File List entry is the exact file name, a plain hyphen, then what it contains.

## Input File List

- evs_cleaning_standards_2026.docx - the department's cleaning task time standards and staffing method: minutes per task, volume basis, shift allocation, productive time and the full-time equivalent conversion, and the costing rules
- unit_4west_room_inventory.csv - the Facilities room inventory for 4 West: each room and common area with its type, square footage, isolation capability and cleaning frequency
- census_projection_4west.xlsx - Decision Support's calendar 2027 projection for 4 West by month: average daily census, discharges per day, isolation share of discharges and occupancy, with the assumptions behind it
- evs_roster_wages_oct2026.xlsx - the Environmental Services roster for the inpatient units with shift, home unit, FTE, base rate and status, and the wage scale with differentials, benefits load and paid hours
- director_memo_4west.docx - the director's memo asking for the plan, with the opening date, the transfers, the approved position, the annual labor budget and her working assumption

## Output File List

- 4west_evs_staffing_plan.xlsx - the 4 West staffing plan workbook with the plan page, the workload by task and shift, the positions and FTE, the cost against the budget, the December peak sensitivity and the note to the director
